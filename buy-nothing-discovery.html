How Radar identifies these opportunities

1. Population Data

Census, health surveys, and demographic shifts. Radar watches population aging, migration, and disease prevalence changes by zipcode.

2. Org Capacity Reports

Each nonprofit submits monthly: clients served, beds full/empty, waitlist length, which populations are underserved.

3. Matching Algorithm

Radar matches surplus to shortage, prioritizing high-impact trades (moves most resources to highest-need areas).

4. Fairness Check

Trade reduces wait times, increases diversity of care, and doesn't exploit any partner. Only then does Radar suggest it.

Interaction flows & user journeys

How community members, donors, and volunteers move through the discovery interface.

Flow 1: Community Member Discovers & Acts

Discovery → Context → Match → Action → Impact Portal

Entry point: Browse happening trades. User clicks a trade, sees the Radar insight (why it matters), then discovers the nonprofit's unmet needs. From there, they choose a specific way to help and move to the Impact Portal for direct engagement.

Flow 2: Donor Researches & Maximizes Impact

Research-heavy path: Dashboard → Segment → Find Nonprofit → Understand Why → Donate → Track

Donor wants to maximize impact. They start at the Impact Dashboard, filter by population (e.g., "Age 55+"), find nonprofits in that area, see the Radar insights explaining why help is urgent, and donate. Results are tracked in the Impact Portal.

Flow 3: Volunteer Sees Urgent Need & Acts

Fast path for urgent action: Browse → Trade → Org → Volunteer → Portal

Volunteers see an urgent need nearby and want to act fast. The path is streamlined: browse, see why it matters (Radar), find the specific need (meal delivery, transportation), and sign up. No friction.

Interaction principles across all flows

1. Context before action

Every action (donate, volunteer, refer) is preceded by understanding why it matters. Radar insights are never hidden behind navigation.

2. Real data grounds decisions

Nonprofits list actual unmet needs, not aspirations. Users see capacity %, waitlist length, and population demand shifts. No guesswork.

3. Multiple entry points

Browse map, search nonprofit, view dashboard, filter by population—each starting point leads to the same core insights and actions.

4. Impact Portal is the doorway

Discovery ends in the Impact Portal. That's where direct engagement, tracking, and long-term relationship building happen.

5. Loop back to explore

Users can compare nonprofits, explore alternative trades, see different subgroups—the system supports research before commitment.

6. Fair prominence to education

"Why fairness matters" and "How Buy Nothing works" are woven throughout, not buried in an FAQ. Stories build trust.
