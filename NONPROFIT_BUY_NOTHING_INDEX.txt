THE CONCEPT IN 2 MINUTES
================================================================================

Problem:
  - Age 55+ unsheltered population rising (29% → 33%)
  - Families unsheltered down 72% (excess capacity somewhere)
  - Senior-focused nonprofits stretched thin
  - Other nonprofits have excess capacity

Traditional fix: Reallocate funding (slow, bureaucratic)

Our fix: Nonprofit Buy Nothing
  - Two nonprofits directly trade excess capacity ↔ unmet need
  - Fairness score ensures both sides benefit
  - Radar data signals where rebalancing matters most
  - Community sees the impact ("500 seniors matched this month")

Example:
  Org A: "We have 50 case management hours, but only 5 seniors to serve"
  Org B: "We have 20 seniors but no case management capacity"
  Fair Trade: Org A's 20 hours ↔ Org B's bed capacity
  Both rate fairness ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐
  Result: 20 seniors get help, both orgs benefit

Scale:
  MVP: 10–15 nonprofits, 10+ trades, manual matching (6 weeks)
  Phase 2: 30–50 nonprofits, 50+ trades/month, automated matching (4 weeks)
  Phase 3: 100+ nonprofits, 200+ trades/month, public launch (ongoing)
